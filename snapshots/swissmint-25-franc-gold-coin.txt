25-franc gold coin
On 1st November 2024, Swissmint will launch the new 25-franc gold coin.
© Swissmint
The 25-franc gold coin from 2024 marks an important moment in Swissmint's history. This coin with its refined design is made from proof quality. The obverse features the Swiss cross and the value 25 FR, accompanied by the legend "CONFOEDERATIO HELVETICA" - a tribute to the classic 25-franc gold coins of 1955, 1956 and 1959. The reverse features the Swiss shield with a cross formed of five equal squares on branches of rhododendron in bloom. The inscription "SWITZERLAND" appears at the top and the year 2024 at the bottom. The design is inspired by the historic 20-franc goldcoin of 1871, and brings a touch of tradition to modern times.
Of particular mention is the inscription "DOMINUS PROVIDEBIT" and 13 stars on the edge, making this the first of the modern 25-franc gold coins. This inscription gives the coin great symbolic value.
With this coin, Swissmint has created an exceptional product - a combination of past and present that not only makes collectors' hearts beat faster.
This piece is not just a testimony to Swiss history, but also a precious collector's item that cannot be ignored. A must-have for anyone who appreciates Swissmint's tradition and craftsmanship.
The coin is issued in a mintage of 5,000 pieces in the quality "Proof" in presentation case.

Questions and answers about purchasing special coins
Can I see the parcel tracking number?
No, the parcel tracking number is not visible in the order.
How can I cancel my subscription?
Cancellation of the subscription must be made in writing, either by post or by email to: info@swissmint.ch. Please state your customer number and full address.
How can I create an account in the online shop?
In the online shop www.swissmintshop.ch, click on the “Sign in/Register” button at the top right of the page. Then click on “CH-Login / agov.ch” and follow the instructions to create an account.
How can I purchase a special coin?
You can purchase Swissmint special coins in the online shop at www.swissmintshop.ch. New special coins are available from 8:00 a.m. on the day of issue.
How can I subscribe to a special coin subscription?
To take out a new Swissmint subscription, simply complete the online form at "subscription".
When will I receive the invoice for the special coins included in my subscription?
Invoices are sent by post approximately 40 days before the issue date. The 30-day payment deadline stated on the invoice must be observed; otherwise, the special coins can no longer be reserved.
Which payment methods for special coins are available in the e-shop?
You can choose from the following payment methods:
With a user account: PostFinance Card, credit card (VISA or Mastercard), TWINT
As a guest: PostFinance Card, credit card (VISA or Mastercard), TWINT
