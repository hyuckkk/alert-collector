What are cookies?
Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They are generally used to make websites function, keep track of your movements within the site, remember your login details, preferences, interests, and more.
Cookies can be distinguished based on their origin, function, and lifespan. Important characteristics of cookies include the following:
First party cookies are placed by the website you are visiting, while third party cookies are placed by a website other than the one you are visiting. Please note that the Company does not control the collection or additional use of data of third parties.
Essential cookies are necessary for the technical operation of the website (e.g., enabling various functions provided during browsing and ensuring service safety in accordance with Article 6, Section 1, Clause 1, Paragraph b (“Performance of a Contract”) of GDPR and Article 6, Section 1, Clause 1, Paragraph f (“Legitimate Interest”)).
Analytical/Performance Cookies collect data on the performance of a website, such as the number of visitors, the time spent on the website and error messages.
Functional and profile cookies remember options you have set while visiting a website, such as language and location settings and login information, to enhance the user convenience of the website.
Advertising / Targeting Cookies enable a website to send you personalized advertising.
Session cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close the browser. On the other hand, permanent cookies remain installed on your access device until you manually delete them or until they are deleted upon the expiration date specified on the cookie file.
More information on all aspects of cookies can be found on www.allaboutcookies.org , Please note that we have no affiliation with, and are not responsible for, this third-party website.
